# Changes

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-06

Adds `healthos-depth-v1.1.0`: 46 optional teaching units across measurements, cardiovascular health, metabolic and liver health, screening and genetics, nutrition, hormones and reproductive health, exercise, pain and movement, sleep, emotional health and habits, research interpretation, and prevention in practice.

Each unit has a stable objective ID, a distilled explanation, source locators, a check and an expected-answer basis. The supplement draws selectively on 57 podcast episodes and four document sources. Repeated material is consolidated by objective; source preferences, historical findings and original examples remain distinguishable.

The skill now routes deeper questions to the relevant chapter and returns to the prior foundation position. New-source guidance distinguishes useful additions, repetition, reference-only material and corrections. Only material changes to previously taught content create pending learner updates.

The five files of `healthos-foundations-v1.0.0` remain unchanged. Its twelve lessons, preventive stance, completion scope and existing learner progress are retained. The supplement ships with the skill and requires no separate source archive, new runtime or database.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-04

Initial portable HealthOS operating method, evidence ledger, prevention review, answer and action guidance, teacher, optional persona and versioned foundation course.

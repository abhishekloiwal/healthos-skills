# Sources for the optional depth release

Release: `healthos-depth-v1.1.0`. This registry records the sources actually used by the selected teaching units. Source lesson/item numbers and timestamps in each chapter refer to the supplied distillation and its recording anchors; those item numbers are editorial locators, not official episode chapter numbers. The lessons themselves include the explanation needed for teaching.

The episodes below are discussions in *The Drive*, hosted by Peter Attia. Attribute guest mechanisms, clinical preferences and interpretations as such. A source discussion is not an independent verification of every study it describes. Historical trial status, products, evidence and recommendations remain dated to the discussion; use current authoritative guidance for personal clinical decisions.

Original content dates are preserved separately from the selected release date when they differ. A rebroadcast or compilation is not fresh independent support. Some source anchors cover a discussion block rather than a precise sentence; the chapter explicitly notes the whole-episode anchor where applicable.

Teaching titles are original editorial work. Examples identify source illustrations or new teaching applications where relevant. Cross-source connections are synthesis, not a claim that speakers jointly endorsed one protocol. The source documents below contribute reasoning exercises; their tables, forms and numerical targets are not bundled.

# Sources and evidence policy

The foundation course is original synthesis. These public sources are starting points for its broad concepts, not frozen substitutes for current local clinical guidance.

## Source order for personal decisions

1. The person's exact original evidence and clinician communications.
2. Current emergency, regulatory, and clinical guidance for the person's jurisdiction.
3. Current recommendations from relevant professional bodies and systematic evidence reviews.
4. The HealthOS foundation course for durable concepts and questions.
5. User-added books, courses, papers, or expert lenses, with provenance and access limits.
6. Clearly labelled agent reasoning where evidence ends.

Recency alone does not establish quality. Record publication or update date, intended population, jurisdiction, evidence method, and conflicts of interest when material.

## Public foundation sources

- World Health Organization, [Guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240014886).
- World Health Organization, [Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet).
- World Health Organization Regional Office for Europe, [A short guide to cancer screening](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789289057561).
- United States Preventive Services Task Force, [Recommendation topics](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation-topics), as one example of a jurisdiction-specific preventive-guidance library.
- United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, [Adult immunization schedule](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult.html), as one jurisdiction-specific schedule that must be checked for updates.
- American Heart Association, [Life's Essential 8](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/healthy-lifestyle/lifes-essential-8), for a public cardiovascular-health construct.
- American Academy of Sleep Medicine and Sleep Research Society, [Recommended amount of sleep for a healthy adult](https://www.aasm.org/resources/pdf/adultsleepdurationconsensus.pdf).
- United States Food and Drug Administration, [Information for consumers on using dietary supplements](https://www.fda.gov/food/dietary-supplements/information-consumers-using-dietary-supplements), for the United States regulatory context.

Users outside the cited jurisdictions should begin with their own health ministry, national screening and immunization programs, regulators, and professional guidance. Even within a jurisdiction, a current condition-specific guideline may be more relevant than a broad public page.

## User-supplied sources

A user's lawful access to a source permits private use only to the extent allowed by law and agreement. It does not automatically permit redistribution, public bundling, model training, persona imitation, or removal of attribution. Keep restricted materials in the user's private workspace and store only the provenance and permissible derivative notes needed for their HealthOS.

When a source is added, record its title, author or organization, edition or date, location, rights or access boundary, intended population, and the topics it informs. Separate faithful notes from the agent's interpretation.
